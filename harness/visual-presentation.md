# Visual presentation preference

Choose the form that makes the explanation easiest to understand. Keep simple answers short.
In a CLI, prefer compact text diagrams for relationships and small tables for comparisons when
they remain readable in a narrow pane. Use a rendered native diagram only when that interface's
rendering is verified. Use temporary local HTML when density or useful interaction warrants it;
identify its link by repository, subject and producing agent. Never open it spontaneously in the
foreground. Visual artifacts need no archive and their temporary links may expire.
